Excel Mini-Project — Analyze, Visualize, and Report

REQUIRED FILE
Continue from DA_Day12_StudentName.xlsx and save a copy as:
DA_Day13_StudentName.xlsx
Keep Student_Data unchanged.

Expected columns:
A Student_ID | B First_Name | C Last_Name | D Gender | E Age
F City | G Course | H Enrollment_Date | I Attendance_Percentage
J Marks | K Fee_Status
Adjust columns and ranges if your workbook differs.



SET UP
- Open the workbook and check Student_Data.
- Create worksheets named Day13_Dashboard and Day13_Report.
- Review your charts and PivotTables from Days 11–12.

TASK A: DATA QUALITY AUDIT
On Day13_Report, create a section named Data Quality Checks. Check and record results for:
1. Blank Student_ID values.
2. Duplicate Student_ID values.
3. Blank or non-numeric Marks values.
4. Blank Attendance_Percentage values.
5. Fee_Status values other than Paid or Pending.

Use filters, COUNTBLANK, conditional formatting, or other suitable methods. For duplicate IDs, if Student_ID is in A2:
=COUNTIF(Student_Data!$A$2:$A$31,A2)
A result greater than 1 means that ID appears more than once in the range.

Do not edit Student_Data. Record any problems and possible fixes in your report.

TASK B: KPI SUMMARY
On Day13_Dashboard, calculate:
- Total students.
- Students who passed (marks >= 40).
- Students who failed (marks < 40).
- Overall average marks.
- Paid fee records.
- Pending fee records.
- Students below the attendance target of 75.

Formula hints, assuming rows 2–31:
Total students: =COUNTA(Student_Data!A2:A31)
Passed: =COUNTIF(Student_Data!J2:J31,">=40")
Failed: =COUNTIF(Student_Data!J2:J31,"<40")
Average marks: =AVERAGE(Student_Data!J2:J31)
Paid: =COUNTIF(Student_Data!K2:K31,"Paid")
Pending: =COUNTIF(Student_Data!K2:K31,"Pending")
Attendance below 75: =COUNTIF(Student_Data!I2:I31,"<75")

If attendance is stored as a true Excel percentage (75% stored as 0.75), use "<75%" instead. Adjust ranges to fit your dataset.

TASK C: VISUAL DASHBOARD
Add at least three charts to Day13_Dashboard, using your Day 11 charts or relevant summary tables:
1. Student Count by Course — column or bar chart.
2. Student Fee Status — bar or pie chart.
3. Student Marks Distribution — bar or column chart.

Dashboard requirements:
- Clear heading: STUDENT DATA ANALYSIS DASHBOARD.
- Meaningful chart titles and readable labels.
- Consistent number formats.
- Avoid unnecessary 3D effects and clutter.
- Align charts and keep KPI values visible.

 TASK D: PIVOTTABLE INSIGHTS
Use at least two PivotTables from Day 12, such as:
- Student count by course.
- Average marks by course.
- Fee status by course.
- Student count by city.

Place or copy the two most useful PivotTables onto Day13_Dashboard, or reference them clearly in the report. Write one observation from each table using actual results.

 TASK E: EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
In Day13_Report, write:
- Three findings supported by calculations or PivotTables.
- One data quality issue found, or a note describing the checks performed if none was found.
- Two practical recommendations based on the data.
- One limitation of the dataset, such as its small practice size.

Do not invent numbers or make unsupported claims.

FINAL REVIEW
- Verify formulas and chart source ranges.
- Check that the dashboard matches the source summaries.
- Refresh PivotTables if the source data changed.
- Confirm report statements match actual results.
- Preserve Student_Data and save the workbook.
