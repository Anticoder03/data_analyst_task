PART 2 — FORMULA PRACTICAL 
In Performance_Summary, calculate or verify:

1. Total students:
   =COUNTA(Student_Data!A2:A31)

2. Total marks:
   =SUM(Student_Data!J2:J31)

3. Average marks:
   =AVERAGE(Student_Data!J2:J31)

4. Lowest marks:
   =MIN(Student_Data!J2:J31)

5. Highest marks:
   =MAX(Student_Data!J2:J31)

6. Number of numeric marks:
   =COUNT(Student_Data!J2:J31)

7. Number of entered first names:
   =COUNTA(Student_Data!B2:B31)

8. Average attendance:
   =AVERAGE(Student_Data!I2:I31)

These examples assume 30 records in rows 2–31. Adjust the ranges if
your dataset contains a different number of records.

For each metric, write one sentence explaining what it tells you.
Verify that formulas reference the correct columns.
