SQL JOINS AND SUBQUERIES
Topics: INNER JOIN, LEFT JOIN, RIGHT JOIN, CROSS JOIN, SELF JOIN, and Nested Queries

OBJECTIVE
Learn how to retrieve and analyse information stored across multiple related tables. Practise SQL joins, understand unmatched records, and use subqueries to answer more complex data questions.
